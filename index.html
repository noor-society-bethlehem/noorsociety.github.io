Who We Are

A Light in the Lives of Children and Families

Noor Society for People with Disabilities is a Palestinian non-profit established in 2011 in Aida Refugee Camp, Bethlehem. We are dedicated to improving the quality of life for children and individuals with disabilities and their families.

With a women-led board (77.8% women) and a decade-long community presence, we are the only organization providing disability-inclusive services with a permanent presence inside Aida Camp.

👁️ Vision

An inclusive society where persons with disabilities live with dignity, equality, and full participation.

🎯 Mission

To empower persons with disabilities through rehabilitation, education, economic empowerment, and protection.

🏛️ Registration

Officially registered · No. BL-3394-C · Palestinian Authority · Bethlehem Governorate

What We Do

Comprehensive Rehabilitation & Protection Services

From clinical therapy to community protection — services adapted for all types of disability.

🧠

Psychosocial Support

Individual and group PSS using Stepping Stones adapted methodology with AAC tools.

🗣️

Speech Therapy

Specialized speech and language therapy for children with autism and communication disorders.

🎵

Music Therapy

Evidence-based music therapy supporting emotional regulation and cognitive development.

🏃

Physiotherapy

Physical rehabilitation supporting mobility, strength, and independence.

✋

Occupational Therapy

Supporting daily living skills, fine motor development, and sensory integration.

🛡️

Protection & Safety

Disability-inclusive protection and community safety networks for women and girls.

📚

Learning Support

Specialized educational support for children with learning difficulties and autism.

💚

Caregiver Empowerment

Monthly workshops, peer support circles, and life skills for mothers and caregivers.
